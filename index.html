DreamResolution

The idea

Each night, a message to your unconscious.

As you fall asleep, you repeat one short intention. Night after night, your unconscious begins working to resolve the issue on inner levels.

  • No need to remember your dreams
  • One minute a night, for thirty nights
  • Nothing leaves your phone

Help and safety

The ideaEach night you send your unconscious the same short message.Repeated as you fall asleep, for thirty nights, it gives your unconscious one clear thing to work on. It begins resolving the issue on inner levels, whether or not you remember your dreams. Your part is only to send the message and notice how you feel.
What this isA reflective practice. It isn’t therapy or medical care, and it doesn’t replace care you already have.
When to talk with a clinician firstIf you’re in crisis, if you have recurring nightmares about a trauma, or if a clinician has advised against sleep-focused practices. For trauma nightmares, imagery rehearsal therapy with a clinician has the strongest evidence.
Statement audioKeep the volume low, or use earbuds if you share a bed. The audio gets softer each time and stops on its own.
DreamsWork with dreams inwardly. Never act them out in waking life.
If you’re in crisis In the US, call or text 988, the Suicide and Crisis Lifeline. Outside the US, find a local helpline.
